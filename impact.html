Programs & Achievements

Impact Delivered in the Field

More than two decades of results across Western Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States.

  • 150,000+

    GBV Prevention & Response

    People reached through GBV prevention and response, including referral pathways and psychosocial support, supported by more than 30 Protection Information Desks across Wau, Jur River, Raja, Bazia, Bagari, and Basilia.

  • 20,000+

    Village Savings & Loans

    People reached through Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) across Western Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States, expanding women's access to credit, savings, and enterprise.

  • 10,000+

    Food Security & Livelihoods

    People reached since 2001 in Bagari, Basilia, and Bazia/Kpaile Payams, and in Tonj North, Awul, Farak Sika, and Kayango Payams.

  • 35+

    Women Lead & Women Rights Forums

    Organizations in the network WDG leads in Western Bahr el Ghazal State, supported by CARE International.

  • 500+

    Ending Early Marriage

    Students and their parents reached with advocacy against early marriage and harmful practices at Hai Salam Primary School and Wau A Girls Primary School.

In Partnership with CARE International

Amplifying Women's Voices

As lead convener of the Women Lead and Women Rights Forums, WDG aligns partner organizations across Western Bahr el Ghazal State, strengthening coordination and amplifying women's voices in local decision-making.

Women Development Group (WDG)

A South Sudanese national NGO empowering women and advancing peace across Western Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States since 1997.
