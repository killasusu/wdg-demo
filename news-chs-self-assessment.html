WDG Completes CHS Self-Assessment, Confirmed by the CHS Alliance

Our formal commitment to quality and accountability to the people we serve.

Women Development Group (WDG) has completed a self-assessment against the Core Humanitarian Standard on Quality and Accountability (CHS), in line with the CHS Alliance Verification Scheme. The CHS Alliance confirmed the assessment on 28 September 2026.

What the CHS means

The Core Humanitarian Standard sets out nine commitments that organizations make to the people and communities they serve: to be accountable, to listen, to involve communities in decisions, and to keep improving. Completing the self-assessment is WDG's formal commitment to these standards.

Putting it into practice

The assessment also produced an improvement plan, which WDG is now putting into practice across its work in Western Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States.

For partners and donors, the CHS confirmation offers independent assurance of how WDG works. For the women, girls, youth and communities WDG serves, it is a promise that their voices shape the programs meant for them.
