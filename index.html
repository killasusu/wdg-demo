Wau, Western Bahr el Ghazal State, South Sudan

Empowering Women, Advancing Peace

The Women Development Group is a South Sudanese national NGO advancing food security, peacebuilding, gender equality, and inclusive governance across Western Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States.

Our Programs Support Our Work
  • 1997 Founded
  • 150,000+ People reached
  • 20,000+ Women in VSLAs
  • 2 States

What We Do

Five Thematic Areas of Impact

  • 01

    Food Security & Economic Empowerment

    Farmer training, market linkages, and Village Savings and Loan Associations.

  • 02

    Peacebuilding & Trauma Healing

    Dialogue, trauma-informed counselling, and community exchange.

  • 03

    Gender Equality & Human Rights

    Advocacy, mentorship, and GBV prevention, monitoring, and referral.

  • 04

    Governance, Participation & Leadership

    Women's and youth participation in decision-making and leadership.

  • 05

    Capacity Strengthening

    Training, mentorship, and exchange for staff, communities, and stakeholders.

Quality & Accountability

Committed to the Core Humanitarian Standard

WDG has completed a CHS Self-Assessment in line with the CHS Alliance Verification Scheme, confirmed by the CHS Alliance on 28 September 2026. It marks our formal commitment to quality and accountability to the people and communities we serve.
