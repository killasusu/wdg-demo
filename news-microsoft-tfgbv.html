Digital Safety · September 2026

A Safer, Smarter WDG

Microsoft for Nonprofits and staff training on technology-facilitated GBV.

WDG has qualified for the Microsoft for Nonprofits program, a major milestone in our digital journey. At the same time, our staff have been trained on Technology-Facilitated Gender-Based Violence (TFGBV).

New tools for our team

Through Microsoft 365, WDG staff now have professional email, Microsoft Teams for meetings and training, OneDrive for secure cloud storage, and SharePoint for managing documents together. These tools make it easier for our office in Wau and our field teams to work as one, while lowering technology costs so more resources go to programs.

Protecting women online

Gender-based violence does not only happen offline. The TFGBV training strengthened our staff's understanding of the online risks facing women, girls and vulnerable communities, and of good practice in privacy, data protection and the safe handling of sensitive information.

Why the two go together

New technology only helps if it is used safely. Linking the training with our move to Microsoft 365 means better protection of sensitive data, stronger account security, and a safer digital environment for the people we serve. The result is a safer, smarter organization that communities can trust with their information.
