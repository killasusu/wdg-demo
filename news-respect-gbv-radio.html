GBV Prevention · July–August 2026

100 Women, Four Communities: ResPEct GBV Radio Project in Wau

Women learning about GBV, referral pathways and economic abuse, and building support networks.

In 2026, WDG joined the second phase of the ResPEct GBV Radio Project, implemented with Media in Cooperation and Transition (MiCT). The project is highly relevant to the context of Western Bahr el Ghazal and South Sudan as a whole.

Listening groups in four communities

WDG held community listening and feedback sessions with 25 women in each of four locations in Wau: Khor Malang, Jebel Khair, Hai Daraja and Hai Lokoloko, reaching 100 women in total. Sessions covered:

  • What gender-based violence is, and the GBV referral pathway
  • Economic abuse, and how women can protect themselves against it
  • Gender inequality, including the harm caused by valuing boys over girls

Women organizing for each other

Women came away understanding where to go for help, and many asked to form women's groups in each location to network on GBV prevention and response. A follow-up plan is now in place to support these groups.

The project also strengthened WDG's own capacity in GBV awareness, and WDG considers it a rich and cost-effective program. We thank MiCT for selecting WDG as a partner.
