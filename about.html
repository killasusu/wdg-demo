About Us

Rooted in Community Since 1997

A South Sudanese national NGO working for women's participation, leadership, and a peaceful, inclusive South Sudan.

Our Mission

WDG is committed to supporting, promoting, and strengthening food security and economic empowerment, as well as peacebuilding and trauma healing, gender equality and human rights, and governance. Our work focuses on women's participation, decision-making, leadership, and capacity building for staff, communities, and other stakeholders, to foster economic and social development and advance social justice.

Our Vision

A peaceful and inclusive South Sudan where women, girls, youth, and vulnerable groups are empowered, protected, and participate in economic and social development and the advancement of social justice.

Our Approach

WDG works alongside communities, government, international NGOs, and UN agencies to design and deliver programs that respond to real local needs.

Rooted in more than two decades of presence in Western Bahr el Ghazal and Warrap States, our approach centers women's participation, decision-making, and leadership, ensuring interventions are community-owned, gender-responsive, and built to last.

Quality & Accountability

Committed to the Core Humanitarian Standard

WDG has completed a CHS Self-Assessment in line with the CHS Alliance Verification Scheme, confirmed by the CHS Alliance on 28 September 2026. It marks our formal commitment to quality and accountability to the people and communities we serve, and we are now putting our improvement plan into practice.
