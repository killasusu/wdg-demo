What We Do

Five Thematic Areas of Impact

Community-owned, gender-responsive programs designed with the people they serve.

  • 01

    Food Security and Economic Empowerment

    We strengthen food security and household incomes through farmer training, agricultural inputs, extension services, market linkages, value addition, and Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs).

  • 02

    Peacebuilding and Trauma Healing

    We restore community cohesion through dialogue, trauma-informed counselling, referral, cultural initiatives, and community exchange, drawing on international, regional, and national peacebuilding frameworks.

  • 03

    Gender Equality and Human Rights

    We advance gender equality and human rights through training, mentorship, advocacy, gender mainstreaming, and GBV prevention, monitoring, and referral, guided by recognized human-rights frameworks.

  • 04

    Governance, Participation and Leadership

    We support women's and youth participation in decision-making and leadership through training, mentorship, coaching, advocacy, community engagement, and women profiling.

  • 05

    Capacity Strengthening for Staff, Community and Stakeholders

    We build the capacities of staff, communities, and stakeholders through needs assessment, on-the-job and community training, mentorship, exchange visits, and research linkages with academic institutions.
