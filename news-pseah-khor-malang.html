Keeping Aid Safe: PSEAH Training in Khor Malang

Community leaders trained to prevent sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment.

Before cash assistance reached 511 of the most vulnerable people in the Khor Malang resettlement site in Jur River County, WDG made sure the community knew how to keep that aid safe.

Training the people who help register families

WDG held a session on the Prevention of Sexual Exploitation, Abuse and Harassment (PSEAH) for the chief, block leaders, and women and youth leaders, the local leaders who take part in registering families for assistance. The session covered:

  • Understanding sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment in a resettlement setting
  • Why people living in resettlement areas face greater risk
  • Core principles: zero tolerance, the duty to report, a survivor-centred approach, and no retaliation

Knowing how to act

Participants gained knowledge of PSEAH and of safe reporting. One community leader said harassment had happened in the past but no action was taken, because people did not know how to report it. After the session, he understood what action to take and which procedures to follow.

This work was supported by the Dutch Relief Alliance (DRA) in partnership with ZOA/Dorcas.
